How We Work

One loop, four parts. We set out what would have to be true, and we show our working.

1 · THE THESIS

Domestic before Continental

What should a place fix at home before it chases regional and continental links? We start at the farm gate and the county, and build outward.

2 · THE METHOD

The Lift Test

Should this airstrip carry cargo, and under what conditions? Seven questions, scored from public information, with the conditions published alongside every score.

3 · THE RECORD

Put the Clock On It

Did the promised thing happen by the date given? We log official pledges, check back on schedule, and give credit when they are met.

4 · THE INVITATION

Corrections Welcome

Authorities, operators and experts check our facts, not our scores. Every correction is logged publicly with its date, and the next score improves.

Note: The Lift Test is an in-house editorial framework developed by Aero Axis Link to analyse air freight economics. It is not an official industry or regulatory metric. Scores are drafts from public information.
