A middling score is not a failure: many strips keep people connected and safe. Authorities validate facts, not scores. Where public evidence is missing, we score conservatively and mark the question; scores rise when the evidence appears.

What would have to be true

  1. A named anchor buyer with steady volume
  2. A confirmed backhaul: cargo that flies out of Garissa
  3. Passengers sharing the cost of the schedule

If these are confirmed, Garissa could move toward 10. That is a condition, not a forecast.

Open questions we would welcome help on

  • Which aircraft flies the route?
  • Is the runway 1.2 km or longer?
  • Is there an export abattoir or tannery planned, and when?
